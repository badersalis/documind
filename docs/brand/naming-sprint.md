This is not legal advice or trademark clearance.** All findings come from public web search, trademark aggregator sites (markinton, Justia, trademarkelite, trademarkdir) and visits to live domains. No official register (OAPI, WIPO Global Brand Database, Madrid Monitor, EUIPO eSearch/TMview, INPI, USPTO TSDR) was searched directly. No domain WHOIS or registrar availability check was run. ## 1. Summary & recommendation

**Recommended ranking (subject to formal search):**

| Rank | Name | One-line rationale | Preliminary risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tikéo** | Built on *ticket (de caisse)*, the everyday Ivorian word for a till receipt. Easy for French and English speakers to say, 3 syllables. No exact-match trademark record or company found. | **Medium-low** |
| 2 | **Recevo** | Built on *reçu* / *recevoir*. Clear meaning in French, sounds natural in Italian and Spanish too. No exact-match record found. Some sound overlap with Receeve (debt-collection SaaS) and REEVO marks. | **Medium** |
| 3 | **Clarto** | Built on *clarté*: bringing clarity and order to expenses. 2 syllables, works in both languages, and clarto.com did not resolve (possibly free). A US CLARTO mark exists in an unrelated class (21, housewares). The name says the least about receipts. | **Medium-low** (conflicts) / weaker on meaning |
| Alt. | **Factalis** | Built on *facture* (invoice). No exact-match conflict found, but factalis.com is a live gambling blog that looks spammy, and FACTORIAL (HR and expense software) sits nearby. | Medium |
| Control | **Documind** | Live USPTO filing in Classes 9/42 (Notice of Allowance per aggregators), 6+ products with the same name, poor domains, meaning not tied to expenses. | **High** |

**Why Tikéo leads.** It is the only candidate that is natural in Ivorian French ("le ticket"), is understood by English speakers, and directly names the object the user photographs. It also passes the screen without an exact-match conflict. **Main weaknesses:**
- To English speakers, "ticket" also means a support ticket or an event ticket.
- The spoken name can be written down as "Tickeo", which is a Czech helpdesk SaaS.
- The accent (é) cannot be used in domains or handles.

**Seeds that were eliminated:**
- **Justif / Justifio:** justif.app is a live French AI receipt-matching app for entrepreneurs and their accountants, a direct overlap. A US JUSTIFI fintech registration also exists.
- **Kauri:** Cauri Money, a fintech in Senegal and Cameroon, has a name that sounds the same.
- **Trezo:** Trezy is French treasury SaaS, and Trezor is a crypto wallet.
- **Resu:** reads as "résumé" in English, and the space is crowded with resume tools.
- **Pièce coinage (Piéco):** pieco.com belongs to a US meat-processing equipment manufacturer.
- **Sika:** dropped per brief because of the Sika AG mark.

**Decision needed from the founder:** choose 1–2 names to send for a formal OAPI + WIPO search (see section 7). Domains, handles and identity work wait until that search is back (D-006).

## 2. Criteria

Applied with the D-011 default, since the founder has not overridden it: **(b) plausibly clearable internationally + (i) meaning tied to expenses, receipts, money or order.**

| # | Criterion | How it was screened |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Plausibly clearable beyond OAPI (US/EU) | Web search plus trademark aggregators for exact and near matches. Focus on Classes 9, 35, 36 and 42, and on finance, accounting, expense and document software in any country |
| C2 | Meaning tied to expenses, receipts, money or order, and natural in French | Author's judgement against Ivorian business vocabulary (`market-research.md` §6: *pièces justificatives, notes de frais, ticket, reçu, facture*) |
| C3 | Easy for francophone West African speakers and English speakers to say | Author's judgement. Spelling-by-ear risk noted. **Not tested with speakers** |
| C4 | Short (ideally 3 syllables or fewer) | Syllables counted in French |
| C5 | No unfortunate meanings | French and English checked by the author. **Dioula, Baoulé, Bété and Nouchi were NOT checked**: this needs native speakers (see section 7) |
| C6 | Reasonable domain options (.com, .ci, .africa, get-/try- prefixes) | Visited each domain. **No WHOIS or registrar check.** A .ci domain that does not resolve is inconclusive |
| C7 | Distinctive enough to register (not merely descriptive in French) | Author's judgement. Purely descriptive words such as *reçu* or *justif* are weak marks for this product |

Risk scale: **Low** = no relevant conflict surfaced · **Medium-low** = conflicts only in unrelated classes or of a different kind · **Medium** = a similar-sounding name in finance or software, or a domain or SEO problem · **High** = the same or nearly the same name in the same or an adjacent field.

## 6. Top 3 with taglines

Taglines describe what the product does, in pre-launch voice. They avoid "100% automatique", accuracy figures and compliance claims (R1, R5). Any mention of SYSCOHADA needs the tax adviser's review before publication.

### 1. Tikéo
- **Main tagline:** « Du ticket de caisse à la dépense classée. » (From till receipt to categorised expense.)
- **Alternative:** « Photographiez vos tickets et factures. Validez. Envoyez à votre comptable. » (Photograph your receipts and invoices. Approve. Send to your accountant.)
- **Accountant path:** « Vos clients envoient leurs tickets, vous recevez des dépenses organisées. » (Your clients send receipts; you receive organised expenses.)
- **Why:** it names the exact object the user photographs, in local everyday language, and keeps a warm, slightly playful tone.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - Leans toward small receipts, so invoices need the tagline to carry them.
  - In English, "ticket" can suggest helpdesk software or events.
  - Spoken word of mouth can turn it into "Tickeo".

### 2. Recevo
- **Main tagline:** « Chaque reçu trouve sa place. » (Every receipt finds its place.)
- **Alternative:** « Reçus et factures en photo ou PDF, rangés en dépenses prêtes pour votre comptable. » (Receipts and invoices as photo or PDF, filed as expenses ready for your accountant.)
- **Why:** it carries the idea of receiving your receipts, sounds slightly Latin and sturdy, and travels to other Romance-language markets.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - Pronunciation varies more in English.
  - It sounds close to Receeve, a finance SaaS.
  - The .com is held on the aftermarket.

### 3. Clarto
- **Main tagline:** « Vos dépenses au clair, pour vous et votre comptable. » (Your expenses, crystal clear, for you and your accountant.)
- **Alternative:** « Photographiez vos reçus. Clarto les classe, vous validez. » (Photograph your receipts. Clarto sorts them; you approve.)
- **Why:** it is the shortest and easiest to say internationally, and the .com may be free. It fits a trust-led "clarity, not magic" stance.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - Says least about receipts or money, so the tagline does more of the work.
  - The "clar-" space is crowded in personal finance.
  - Writing it as "Klarto" leads to an existing mark owner.

## 7. Next steps (nothing below has been done yet)

1. **Founder:** pick 1–2 names from the shortlist and answer the approval questions below.
2. **IP agent (OAPI/WIPO), founder-funded:**
   - Formal search for the chosen name(s) at OAPI, covering Classes 9, 35, 36 and 42, plus similar-sounding marks.
   - WIPO Global Brand Database and Madrid Monitor.
   - EUIPO/TMview and INPI.
   - USPTO TSDR, specifically for Tickeo/TIKEO, Receeve/REEVO and CLARTO depending on the choice.
   - Advice on whether to file at OAPI first (first-to-file; one filing covers all 17 member states).
   - The cost estimate of a few hundred USD is an assumption, not a quote.
3. **Language check:** 3–5 native speakers each of Dioula, Baoulé, Bété and Nouchi say and react to the chosen name(s). Record any meanings, sound-alikes or jokes. Also check Wolof, Fon and Mossi if regional rollout is planned.
4. **Ear test:** say each name aloud to 10 people (WhatsApp voice note) and ask them to type it. This measures how often it gets misspelled (Tickeo, Klarto, Resevo).
5. **Domains and handles:**
   - **Only after founder approval and after the formal search comes back clear.**
   - Run WHOIS or registrar checks for .com, .ci (NIC.CI), .africa and get-/try- prefixes.
   - Check handles on Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, TikTok and GitHub.
   - Any purchase, including aftermarket .com prices, needs founder approval (brief: no paid services without approval).
6. **Update records:** close D-011 with the founder's choice, record the chosen name in the decision log, and only then unblock identity work (deliverable 2).
