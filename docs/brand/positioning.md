_Prepared 2026-11-23 · Phase 1 · Owner: Director · Status: **Option A approved by founder 2026-11-23 (D-012)**; product promises in §10 pending · Name-agnostic: "[Nom]" stands for the final name (see `naming-sprint.md`)_

**Inputs:** `project-brief.md`, `research/market-research.md`, `research/competitor-analysis.md`, `research/customer-personas.md`, decisions D-003, D-008 and D-009.
**Evidence basis:** desk research only. No customer interviews have been done. Every statement below is a **hypothesis to test**, not a validated insight. Copy is in French (the launch locale), with an English gloss in italics.

---

## 1. Recommendation in one paragraph
Position [Nom] as **the bridge between the pile of receipts and the accountant**. It turns expense documents (receipts, supplier invoices, paper or photo) into organised, reviewable data that is ready for the accountant, Sage or Excel, and it is built for Côte d'Ivoire (French, FCFA, SYSCOHADA). Lead with **order and time** (the outcome), use **SYSCOHADA/local fit** as the main proof of difference, and use **"you stay in control"** (human review, transparency) as the trust pillar. Avoid AI-first, "automatic" and compliance language altogether.

### Positioning statement
> **For** owner-managers, independents and their accountants in Côte d'Ivoire **who** lose hours re-keying and sorting receipts and supplier invoices, **[Nom] is** an expense-document tool **that** turns paper and photos into organised expenses, sorted into SYSCOHADA categories and ready for the accountant. **Unlike** all-in-one ERPs that ask you to change everything, or foreign tools built for euros and the French chart of accounts, **[Nom]** fits alongside how you already work (your accountant, Sage, Excel) and keeps you in control of every entry.

## 7. Brand personality and tone principles

**Personality:** *le collaborateur rigoureux*, the meticulous colleague. Calm, precise, local and honest. Closer to a trusted accountant than to a Silicon Valley startup.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Clair avant tout** *(Plain first)* | Short sentences; the outcome before the technology; concrete nouns (reçu, facture, comptable) | Jargon, "IA" in headlines, English buzzwords (« workflow », « smart ») |
| **Professionnel et proche** *(Professional, approachable)* | Vouvoiement; warm but sober; Ivorian business vocabulary (pièces justificatives, FCFA, notes de frais) | Tutoiement, slang, Parisian-startup register, or a stiff administrative tone |
| **Honnête sur les limites** *(Honest about limits)* | Say what is in development; say that the AI suggests and you validate; answer the hard FAQ questions directly | Overclaiming, vague "sécurité maximale", hiding pre-launch status |
| **Rassurant, pas alarmiste** *(Reassuring, not fear-based)* | FNE and VAT as context and a reason for order | Fear marketing about tax audits or penalties |
| **Local sans folklore** *(Local without clichés)* | Real local documents, places and payment habits | Stereotyped "African" imagery or a token-pattern aesthetic (relevant for visual direction) |

## 10. Trade-offs and open points
- **Order vs AI story:** we give up "AI excitement" in exchange for trust and clarity. This suits a financial-document product and a sceptical, pre-launch audience (H4).
- **SYSCOHADA prominence:** it strongly differentiates for accountants and structured SMBs but may mean little to independents. It goes in the hero subline, not the H1, until tested.
- **Product promises the site would make.** Before any of the following appears in copy, the **founder must confirm each is intended in the product**: (1) SYSCOHADA category mapping; (2) human review of each entry; (3) export to the accountant / Sage / Excel; (4) WhatsApp intake (not used in the recommended copy). These are research-led, not founder-stated.
- **Name dependency:** none of this relies on the name. A name with a French expense meaning (D-011 default) would reinforce pillar 2.
