# Name check: "Documind"

**Type:** Preliminary scan using public web search only. This is not legal clearance.
**Date checked:** 2026-11-23 (brand-creative-director agent; Director review pending founder decision D-006)
**Scope:** Existing companies and products, public signs of trademark filings, domains, social handles, and a language check (French and English).

---

## Summary verdict: **Reconsider** (or proceed with caution if the launch stays limited to OAPI countries)

**Why:**

1. **There is a live US trademark filing for the exact word in the exact category.** DOCUMIND, USPTO serial 99570015, was filed on 2025-12-29 by an individual applicant. It covers Class 9 (downloadable AI software for document understanding and processing) and Class 42 (AI document-processing SaaS). Third-party trademark sites show it was published on 2026-05-26 and received a Notice of Allowance, last updated 2026-07-21. A Notice of Allowance means the office has accepted the mark and it can register once the applicant proves it is in use. This is the same goods and services area as our product. It does not block an OAPI filing for Côte d'Ivoire on its own, because trademarks are territorial. It would, however, be a serious obstacle to the "possibly international" plan, at least in the US. *(Status comes from aggregators; not confirmed in USPTO's own system, TSDR.)*
2. **The name is already crowded.** At least six unrelated products called Documind or DocuMind are active or recently active in document AI. They include an open-source extraction tool with about 1.5k GitHub stars that does almost exactly what we do: structured data extraction from PDFs and images. The result is weak distinctiveness, hard SEO, and confusion in app stores and on social media.
3. **The domains are poor.** documind.ai is listed for sale at USD 16,999. documind.app is a live document product with a German link. documind.chat and documind-ai.com belong to other products. documind.com did not return any content.
4. **The name says little about what we do.** It reads as general "document intelligence", not expenses, receipts or accounting. The "mind" part is English and many francophone users will not understand it naturally.

Two things are still unknown: nothing was found in OAPI, and Côte d'Ivoire itself is not verified. If the founder strongly prefers this name and expects to stay in OAPI countries for several years, a formal OAPI search could still clear it locally. Even then, the international and distinctiveness costs remain.

## Trademark observations

**What was found publicly:**
- **USPTO:** DOCUMIND, SN 99570015, filed 2025-12-29, Classes 9 and 42. Published 2026-05-26. Notice of Allowance per third-party aggregators. **Not confirmed in TSDR.**
- **USPTO:** similar marks exist, including DOCUMINER (registered), DOCBRAIN and DOCUMASTER.
- **OAPI** (the regional IP office covering Côte d'Ivoire and 16 other member states; one filing covers all of them): a web search of OAPI's trademark bulletins (BOPI) found **no** visible match. Search engines only index the tables of contents of these PDFs, so this is **not** meaningful evidence that no filing exists.
- **EUIPO / WIPO (Madrid):** no public DOCUMIND record surfaced in search results.

**What was NOT verified:**
- OAPI's register, searched directly (Classes 9, 35, 36, 42; similar-sounding marks; unpublished filings)
- WIPO Global Brand Database and Madrid Monitor
- EUIPO eSearch, TMview, and France's INPI register
- The US application's status in TSDR, and whether it was also filed abroad
- Common-law rights from actual use by the products listed above
- Company registers (CEPICI/RCCM in Côte d'Ivoire, and others)
- Domain WHOIS data and whether domains are really free to register

## Language notes
- **English:** "docu" plus "mind". Clear but generic document-AI naming. Nothing about expenses, money or receipts.
- **French:** "Docu" reads naturally. "Mind" is not French, so it carries no meaning for francophone users who don't speak English. No negative meaning found. Meanings in local languages (Dioula, Baoulé, Bété, Nouchi and others) were **not verified**.
- **Pronunciation:** expect several versions (do-ku-MAÏND / do-ky-MIND / do-ky-MINDE). Spelling it out by ear may also be unreliable (Documine, Documaind). This matters for word of mouth and WhatsApp sharing.

## Recommendations

1. **Don't invest in identity work** (logo, domain purchase, printed materials) until an IP professional has run a formal search covering OAPI (Classes 9, 35, 36, 42), WIPO/Madrid, EUIPO/INPI, and the status of USPTO SN 99570015.
2. **If the founder keeps Documind:** file at OAPI early (first-to-file; one filing covers all 17 states). Accept that US expansion may need a different name. Domain fallbacks: documind.ci, documind.africa, getdocumind.com, trydocumind.com. Make the tagline say what the product does.
3. **Alternative: run a short renaming sprint.** Starting directions only. **None has been screened.**
   - **Kauri:** cowrie shell, a historical West African currency
   - **Justif / Justifio:** from *justificatif* (the French accounting word for a supporting receipt)
   - **Reçu-based coinage (Resu, Recevo):** from *reçu* (receipt)
   - **Factalis:** *facture* (invoice) plus analysis
   - **Trezo:** from *trésorerie* (cash management)
   - **Pièce-based coinage:** from *pièce comptable* (accounting voucher)
   - **Sika:** "gold" or "money" in Akan languages. **High conflict risk with the Sika AG mark.** For discussion only.
4. **Questions for the founder:** How important is international/US expansion within 3 years? Is there budget for a formal OAPI and WIPO search (estimated at a few hundred USD through an agent; this cost is an assumption)? Should the name signal expenses and money, or document intelligence in general?

*Preliminary desk scan. Not legal advice or trademark clearance.*
