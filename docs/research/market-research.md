# Market Research: Documind, Côte d'Ivoire

_Prepared 2026-11-23 · Phase 1 (Positioning and discovery) · Author: Business Strategist agent · Status: desk research only. No customer interviews have been run. Director note: sources have not been independently re-checked by the Director. Treat M/L items as leads, and re-verify any figure before using it in public copy._

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **The regulatory moment is the strongest hook available, and it is time-sensitive.** The DGI's Facture Normalisée Électronique (FNE), with its electronic receipt counterpart RNE, was launched on 21 July 2025 [8]. The 2025 tax annex extends it to all economic operators [9]. Nationwide DGI compliance checks have been running since 1 September 2026 across the RNI, RSI, RME and entreprenant regimes [10]. Traders' federations are pushing back publicly [11]. Businesses and accountants are being forced to digitise invoicing right now. One distinction matters for messaging: FNE governs **issuing sales invoices**, while Documind handles **incoming expense documents**. Copy must make the link without implying that Documind is an FNE solution.
- **SYSCOHADA is the language of the target buyer.** The revised SYSCOHADA (Acte uniforme of 26 January 2017) has applied to individual accounts since 1 January 2018 [12]. Small entities below set turnover thresholds may use the cash-based Système Minimal de Trésorerie (SMT) [13]. "Catégories SYSCOHADA" will signal local relevance far better than "expense categories". International tools reportedly lack it [21, low confidence].
- **The market is large but mostly informal. The addressable formal market is unquantified.** About 89% of jobs were informal (ENE 2019) [3]. The informal sector was estimated at about 47.5% of GDP (2015–2017) [4]. CEPICI registered 26,948 new companies in 2025 [5]. The INS business census (RGEE-CI) was launched in 2024, but no results were found [4]. Only about 280 chartered accountants were registered with the Ordre in October 2024 [6]. That is a small, concentrated channel and plausibly overloaded.
- **Digital context.** The site should be mobile-first but must also work well on desktop. Internet penetration is 40.7% (13.4M users, late 2025) and mobile connections equal 151% of the population [1]. StatCounter web traffic is close to evenly split (October 2026: 53.0% mobile, 46.5% desktop) [15], and the split moves a lot from month to month. B2B accounting work probably skews to desktop (inference).
- **Mobile money is the payment rail.** CI's financial inclusion rate was 88.7% in 2025, driven by e-money across the UEMOA region [17]. Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov Money and Wave are licensed e-money issuers [18].
- **Privacy rules are stricter than many founders assume.** Under Law No. 2013-450, processing personal data generally requires **prior declaration to ARTCI**. Transfers to third countries require **prior ARTCI authorisation**, even to "adequate" countries [19]. A waitlist stored on foreign-hosted services (for example Vercel or Supabase outside CI) is likely in scope. This must go to a legal specialist before launch.

**Bottom line:** Position Documind in French as a tool that brings order to expense documents in a SYSCOHADA world that is going digital through FNE. Lead with time saved and accountant-ready output. Treat accountants as a priority segment to validate. Make trust, data handling and honest "pre-launch" signals central to the site.

## 4. Market signals

### Demand drivers
- **FNE/RNE enforcement (E16–E20).** This is a forced digitisation event. Every regime, including micro-businesses and entreprenants, must now issue electronic invoices, and controls are live. *Inference:* businesses are actively thinking about invoices and compliance, and accountants are dealing with new formats. FNE invoices carry a QR code and continuous electronic numbering [9]. Received FNE invoices may therefore be more structured and machine-readable than legacy paper. This is a potential product advantage, to be validated technically later.
- **e-impots and online VAT filing (E22–E23).** Filing deadlines create recurring monthly pressure to have expenses and deductible VAT organised. *Inference:* "be ready for your monthly VAT return" is a plausible trigger.
- **Business formation.** About 27,000 new companies a year (E12), and online registration in about 2.4 days [5]. Each is a new bookkeeping obligation.
- **Accountant scarcity (E13).** Around 280 chartered accountants for a country of 32.9M. *Inference:* firms and agréé/independent bookkeepers likely carry heavy client loads, so data-entry productivity has value. The number of non-chartered bookkeepers and comptables agréés was not found.

### Constraints
- **Informality (E9–E10).** Most economic activity has no formal books, and many expenses lack receipts. The serviceable market is the formal and semi-formal segment, whose size is unknown until RGEE-CI results are published.
- **Low digital accounting baseline (E15).** About a quarter of surveyed African firms use digital tools for accounting. That signals both the opportunity and the need for education.
- **Payments.** SaaS card payment habits are not documented (not found). Mobile money dominates (E26–E28).

### Trends
- Mobile money leadership (CI is the largest UMOA e-money market, E27). Wave has joined the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment system [17c].
- The Startup Act and its 2026 tax incentives (E33), and government programmes (Ivoire Tech Next 15 / Scale Up, July 2026) [25b]. These are possible support channels and credibility labels for Documind later.
- An emerging AI conversation among Ivorian SMEs (Koaci, 2026-11-19) [26]. This is anecdotal.

### Substitutes and alternatives (preliminary; full analysis in `competitor-analysis.md`)
Paper and shoeboxes; Excel; outsourced bookkeeping (accounting firms, Big-4-adjacent firms such as Forvis Mazars CI [27]); desktop or cloud SYSCOHADA software (Sage 100 OHADA, iCompta, OmniCompta, per a vendor blog [21]); custom OCR projects (one vendor quotes 3–9M FCFA setup plus 25–80 FCFA per document [21], L); international receipt scanners (no SYSCOHADA support, often English-only, per a vendor claim [21], to be verified).

### Regulation (marketing-site relevant)
- **Law 2013-450 / ARTCI (E29–E31).** Verified points: the law exists, ARTCI is the authority, there is a prior declaration regime, third-country transfers need prior authorisation, and data subjects have a right to object to prospecting. Unclear: whether a small waitlist triggers a full declaration or a simplified one; practical ARTCI timelines and fees; whether an Ivorian legal entity is required to declare (E30 suggests one is required for transfers); and how foreign-hosted form or email providers fit. **Flag for legal specialist before launch.**
- **Law 2013-546 (electronic transactions).** Electronic direct marketing needs prior consent. The exact article and its exceptions were not verified against the text. Use explicit opt-in.

### Adoption barriers (hypotheses unless sourced)
1. Trust in uploading financial documents to the cloud and to AI (not measured; H4).
2. Data-location concerns, which are legally real (E30).
3. Document quality: thermal, handwritten and stamped receipts [21, L]. No independent data.
4. Price sensitivity and the payment method. No CI SaaS pricing data found.
5. Fit with accountants' existing tools (Sage, Excel) and SYSCOHADA output.
6. Informality: no receipt, nothing to classify.

## 6. Implications for positioning, messaging and the marketing website

**Language**
- French is the default locale (route `/fr` or root), written in natural Ivorian business French (for example "pièces justificatives", "notes de frais", "factures fournisseurs", "comptable", "FCFA"). English is secondary, for diaspora, investors and regional expansion. H1 stays as is, now supported by the context.
- Use local vocabulary in the copy: **SYSCOHADA**, **TVA 18%**, **FCFA/XOF**, **NCC**, **FNE**, **e-impots**. *Inference:* local terms signal that the product was built for Côte d'Ivoire, which is the main differentiator against global tools.

**Positioning hooks (hypotheses for brand positioning)**
1. *SYSCOHADA-native:* "Vos factures et reçus classés selon le plan SYSCOHADA." Only use this once the product intent is confirmed. In pre-launch copy, phrase it as a goal ("conçu pour…").
2. *FNE context, without claiming compliance:* "La facturation passe à l'électronique. Vos dépenses aussi." Never state or imply that Documind issues FNE invoices, is DGI-approved, or guarantees tax compliance (brief R1).
3. *Accountant leverage:* "Moins de saisie, plus de conseil": recover hours per client file. The accountant segment deserves its own Use Case page section, because a scarce profession has high leverage.
4. *Monthly VAT readiness:* organised expenses and deductible VAT before the e-impots deadline. Phrase this as a benefit, with no promise of correctness.

**Mobile-first, desktop-strong**
- Traffic is split roughly 50/50 (E8). Design from 320 px upwards, but give desktop layouts, aimed at accountants, equal polish.
- Set a performance budget for mid-range Android phones and variable 4G: minimal JavaScript, compressed images, no autoplay video, and system or subset fonts. Chrome dominates browser share [15].
- Show the "how it works" flow as photo of receipt, then extracted fields, then category, using thermal and handwritten receipt visuals that look local.

**Trust signals (central, H4)**
- A plain-language privacy notice in French. State where waitlist data is stored, and do not over-promise.
- No compliance, certification or "ARTCI-approved" claims until verified (brief non-goal).
- Honest pre-launch framing: "En cours de développement – rejoignez la liste d'attente".
- A founder or "made in Abidjan" story, local contact details and a local phone number, if the founder agrees.
- An FAQ covering: Where are my documents stored? Who can see them? Does the AI make mistakes? Does it replace my accountant? Is it compatible with SYSCOHADA / Sage / Excel?

**Waitlist form and capture**
- Keep fields to a minimum: email (required); role (SMB / freelancer / accountant / finance team), as a segmentation signal for validation; and an optional WhatsApp number, given likely channel preference (E32, low confidence; founder input U9).
- Use an explicit, unticked opt-in checkbox for future communications (Law 2013-546). Include a link to the privacy notice and a statement of the right to object.
- **Before launch:** a legal check on ARTCI declaration and transfer authorisation for the chosen storage and email provider (D-004). Where possible, prefer storage that minimises cross-border transfers, or obtain legal sign-off.

**Channels (inference)**
WhatsApp click-to-chat as a secondary contact option. LinkedIn (1.8M members, E5) for accountants and finance teams. Facebook (8.4M reach) for SMBs and freelancers. Accountant-association events (OECCI) as a partnership route.

**Pricing page**
Stays excluded. When it is created, show prices in FCFA, plan for mobile money, and consider per-document or per-client-file pricing for accountants (hypothesis).

## 7. What must be validated directly with customers

1. **Pain intensity.** How many receipts and invoices per month, and how many hours of entry and categorisation, for each segment? Who does it today (owner, assistant, external accountant)?
2. **Segment priority.** Are accountants or firms the best beachhead (H3)? Do SMB owners hand everything to their accountant anyway?
3. **Document reality.** What share is thermal, handwritten, stamped, FNE (QR) or PDF? How are documents received (WhatsApp photos, email, paper)?
4. **Output needed.** SYSCOHADA account codes, SMT cash journal, Sage/Excel export, VAT breakdown?
5. **FNE effect.** Has FNE changed how they receive and store supplier invoices? Does it raise or reduce the pain?
6. **Trust.** Willingness to upload financial documents to the cloud and AI; data-location expectations; deal-breakers.
7. **Willingness to pay and payment method.** Price anchors in FCFA; mobile money vs card; monthly vs per-document.
8. **Language and channel.** French only, or French/English; WhatsApp vs email for onboarding and support.
9. **Message test.** 5-second test of 2–3 headline variants: SYSCOHADA-led, time-saving-led, accountant-led.

Method suggestion: 5–10 founder interviews per priority segment (a minimum of 15 total), plus waitlist role-split data and A/B headline tests after launch.
