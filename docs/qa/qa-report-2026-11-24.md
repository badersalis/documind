## 3. Checks that passed without changes
- **Structure (all 8 pages):** exactly one `<h1>`; no skipped heading levels; no duplicate ids; every decorative SVG hidden from assistive tech; `lang="fr-CI"`; skip link to `#contenu`.
- **SEO:** unique French `<title>` (24–68 characters) and description on every page; canonical URL; Open Graph image; `sitemap.xml`; `robots.txt` disallows all and pages send `noindex` until `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_LIVE=true`.
- **Links:** all internal links resolve (200); no broken links.
- **Contrast (text):** every text colour pair used passes AA, including semi-transparent body text: ink/80 9.6:1, muted on receipt grey 5.6:1, light blue text on the blue band 6.2:1, blue on blue tint 6.9:1, error red 6.6:1.
- **Motion:** the marker sweep and stamp are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Targets:** buttons, menu items, FAQ rows, chips and mobile footer links are ≥ 44 px.
- **Layout:** 40 page × width combinations (320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 px) scanned for elements wider than the viewport: none after Q9. Wide tables scroll inside their own box.
- **Privacy by construction:** no third-party requests from visitors (fonts self-hosted), no cookies, no analytics; the waitlist runs in contact-only mode by default.
- **Validation logic:** email, role and Ivorian WhatsApp normalisation (10 digits, `+225`/`00225`/local forms) pass 11 test cases.

## 7. Addendum: waitlist form in `live` mode, tested locally (2026-11-24, later)

Found while capturing the form's states for a social piece (dev server, `NEXT_PUBLIC_WAITLIST_MODE=live`, `WAITLIST_STORE=dev-log`, Edge, 390 px). This covers part of §6 item 3. Findings 11–13 are fixed and 14 is open; `tsc` and `eslint` pass.

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Invisible keyboard focus inside the form.** `.on-stamp :focus-visible` paints a white ring, and the form is a white card inside the blue section, so the ring was white on white (WCAG 2.4.7). Also affected the contact card and the success card. | High (a11y) | `.on-stamp .on-paper :focus-visible` restores the blue ring. `on-paper` added to the form and the success card (`WaitlistForm.tsx`) and to the contact card (`WaitlistBlock.tsx`). Verified: computed outline `3px solid rgb(31,63,209)`. |
| 12 | **First press on "Rejoindre" did nothing** after typing an invalid WhatsApp number (or e-mail). Mousedown blurred the field, the blur check rendered the error, the button moved down by about 60 px, and the mouseup landed off it. | Medium | Blur validation is skipped when focus moves to the submit button (`onBlurCheck`); `onSubmit` validates everything anyway. Verified: one press now shows all three errors and focuses the e-mail field; blur to the next field still validates. |
| 13 | **The hero's marker sweep never showed, and the highlights had pure-yellow edges.** `.marker` set a gradient but not `background-color`, so the browser's default `<mark>` yellow (#FF0) filled the soft ends and sat under the animation from frame one. | Medium (brand signature motion) | `background-color: transparent` on `.marker` (`globals.css`). Verified with paused frames: nothing at 0 ms, half-swept at 300 ms, brand yellow when done. |
| 14 | The marker stagger (`.marker:nth-of-type(2/3)` delays) never applies: each `<mark>` is the only one in its parent, so all three sweep together. | Low | **Open.** Fix with an inline `animation-delay` per index in `Receipt.tsx` if the stagger matters. |
